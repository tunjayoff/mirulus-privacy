Mirulus Studio

The private production tool behind the Mirulus channel on YouTube.

Mirulus Studio is used only by the channel's owner. It plans and produces the channel's short videos and shows the owner how they perform. When the owner connects their Google account, the tool reads the channel's own YouTube statistics and analytics (read-only) so they can be shown on a local dashboard.

It is not offered to other users and does not upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.
