Privacy policy

Mirulus Studio · last updated 17 November 2026 (comment replies added)

Who uses it

Mirulus Studio is a personal tool run by the owner of the Mirulus channel on their own computer. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, it requests read-only access to the channel's videos (youtube.readonly) and YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly: views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, countries), and youtube.force-ssl, which it uses for one thing only: posting a reply to a comment on the channel's own videos after the owner has read, edited and approved that reply. It never uploads, edits or deletes videos, playlists or comments.

To help the owner reply, the text of public comments on the channel's videos is read and drafts are written with an AI model; nothing is posted without the owner's approval.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to show the owner how the channel's videos perform. It is stored on the owner's own computer, is never sold, shared or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising. Mirulus Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect at any time from the tool's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions; stored data can be deleted from the owner's computer.
